Accessibility Statement

Jubilee Prosthetics & Orthotics is committed to making this website usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technology.

What this site aims to do

  • Provide semantic HTML structure and a logical heading hierarchy.
  • Support keyboard navigation throughout, including menus, forms, the FAQ accordion and the before/after slider.
  • Maintain visible focus states and sufficient color contrast.
  • Respect your device's reduced-motion setting.
  • Provide meaningful alt text on images once final photography is added.
