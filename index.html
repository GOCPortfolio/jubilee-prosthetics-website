ABOUT JUBILEE

A dedicated partner in your mobility journey.

Jubilee Prosthetics & Orthotics is a specialized organization focused on improving mobility, independence and quality of life for people living with limb loss, physical disabilities and musculoskeletal conditions.

Every service is guided by four principles: Compassion. Excellence. Innovation. Integrity.

WHY JUBILEE

Care shaped by seven values.

MOBILITY, REIMAGINED

Movement means something different for everyone.

For some, it means walking a child to school. For others, it means returning to work, to sport, or simply moving through a day with more confidence. Jubilee's role is to support that meaning — whatever it looks like for you.

Every plan is personal. Outcomes vary by individual circumstance, and Jubilee's team will discuss what's realistic for you during an assessment.

THE PROCESS

How Jubilee helps.

A general overview of what to expect. Your specific care plan will be discussed with Jubilee's clinical team.

  1. 01

    Talk to Jubilee

    Reach out by phone, WhatsApp, email or the appointment form to share what brings you to Jubilee.

  2. 02

    Assessment

    Jubilee's clinical team reviews your needs and mobility goals.

  3. 03

    Personalized Solution

    A plan is discussed based on your assessment and circumstances.

  4. 04

    Fitting & Support

    Your device or orthosis is fitted with guidance on use and care.

  5. 05

    Follow-up & Ongoing Care

    Jubilee remains available for adjustments, repairs and continued support.
