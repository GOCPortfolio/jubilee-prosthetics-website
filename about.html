ABOUT JUBILEE

Care built on compassion and clinical excellence.

Jubilee Prosthetics & Orthotics is a specialized organization focused on improving mobility, independence and quality of life for people living with limb loss, physical disabilities and musculoskeletal conditions.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, innovative, and patient-centered prosthetic and orthotic services.

Our Vision

To become a leading center of excellence in prosthetics and orthotics, and transform lives.

What guides Jubilee's care.

OUR STORY

Turning challenges into triumph.

Jubilee's name reflects its purpose: a moment of celebration for every person who moves toward greater independence. The brand identity brings together a stylized "J," a cross, and the image of a champion — representing greatness, independence, ability and championship.

Jubilee exists to help people move toward greater mobility, independence and quality of life through professional prosthetic and orthotic services.
